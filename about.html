PROJECT INFORMATION

ABOUT CORE_SEARCH

CORE_SEARCH is a browser-based search dashboard that brings web search, bookmarks, browser preferences, and optional web access tools together in one place. The project is actively developing, so some sites or features may not work consistently.

TOOLS

Choose DuckDuckGo, Google, or Bing for searches. Use the built-in address bar and tabs to open websites through the available proxy service.

The browser profile in Settings selects the connection flow: Safari opens a full-page photo page; other profiles use the embedded Scramjet browser.

PRIVACY & LIMITATIONS

Your theme, browser profile, search preference, and bookmarks are stored in this browser. Searches go to the search engine you select. When you use a proxy, your request passes through that proxy service before reaching the destination website.

CORE_SEARCH does not promise anonymity or compatibility with every site. Avoid entering passwords, payment information, or other sensitive data into proxied pages.

WHAT WE PROVIDE

Our goal is to ensure you can use the Internet the way you want to, safely and securely. We try our best to provide a seamless and secure browsing experience through our tools and services. CORE_SEARCH is designed to be ad-free. We don't allow such interruptions on our websites, ever. CORE_SEARCH is a free service, and it's going to stay that way.

We try our best to block malware from ever appearing in our embedded browsers. If you encounter any malware, PLEASE fot God's sake, LET US KNOW! We want to protect you as best as we can.
